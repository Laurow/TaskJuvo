# TaskJuvo — prototype expert review

**Goal:** check whether a small business owner can describe a clear task, compare suitable students and understand the agreed outcome. Allow 20–30 minutes per session. Use fictional business details.

## Before the session

Say: “This is a clickable concept. Please think aloud; we are testing the design, not you.” Let the participant work independently first. Help only when they get stuck, and record that assistance. For each task, note: completed without help / with help / not completed, moments of uncertainty and one direct quote.

## Scenario and tasks

You own a small business planning to expand into Germany. Your team has little time for research. You need a competitor analysis within **10 days**, with a total budget of **€300**. The required skills are **Market research, Excel and PowerPoint**.

1. **Start on the homepage.** Explain in your own words what TaskJuvo does, who it serves and what you would do first.
2. **Post a task.** Use the title “Competitor analysis for expansion into Germany”. Request an overview of five competitors, an Excel pricing comparison, positioning advice and a final PowerPoint presentation. Enter the budget, deadline and three skills.
3. **Review your task.** Change one detail and check the summary. Then find matching talent.
4. **Compare the three matches.** Explain whom you would choose and why. Open your strongest candidate’s profile. Identify which evidence is convincing and explain what “TaskJuvo Verified” means to you.
5. **Review the project confirmation.** Check the talent, price, deadline, deliverables and milestones. Explain what you think the Project Guarantee promises. Confirm the example project.
6. **Explore the workspace.** Identify the current status, the next milestone and where you would contact the student or request support. Review the agreement and try the example actions.

Use **Fill in example** only after the independent attempt, or to leave more time for the remaining screens. Record when it is used.

Ask the participant to rate each statement from **1 (not at all) to 5 (completely)** and explain their rating:

Then ask: “What information is missing before you would really use this?”, “What would you still need to supervise yourself?” and “Which single change would remove the most uncertainty?” Ask coaches which claims or terms need more evidence.

## Accessibility and clarity

- **Keyboard:** follow the main flow with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and space. Is focus visible and logical? Can you reach every field, skill choice and action? Close dialogs with Escape and check that focus returns.
- **Forms:** try an empty required field and invalid practical details. Is the error understandable, associated with the field and recoverable without losing entered data?
- **Screen reader:** check page titles, headings, field labels, selected choices, the current step and announced status changes. Are icons and percentages understandable without seeing the screen?
- **Mobile and zoom:** review at widths of 390px and 320px, and at 200% zoom. Check text, buttons, summaries and comparisons for clipping, overlap or unnecessary horizontal scrolling.
- **Visibility:** assess contrast, readable text and touch targets. Status and selection should also be recognizable through text or shape. Check the experience with reduced motion enabled.

Record each issue with its screen, action, expected result, actual result and severity: **blocks completion**, **causes difficulty** or **minor improvement**. This review is not a complete WCAG audit or a statement of conformance.

## Prototype limits

Students, tasks, reviews, match scores, project evidence and progress are fictional examples. Matching uses preset demonstrations. “TaskJuvo Verified” and the Project Guarantee describe concepts to validate; no real verification or binding guarantee is provided. There are no real payments, accounts, agreements, delivered messages or support requests.

Task details, selected talent, feedback and example messages can remain in this browser after reloading. Example milestone progress may reset when the workspace is reopened. Use **Start again** before a new participant. Download feedback before resetting: it clears local demo storage. Keep research notes separately and obtain permission before recording a session.
